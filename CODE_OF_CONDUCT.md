# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

SwiftRide welcomes collaboration from people with varied backgrounds, identities, skills, and experience. We aim to keep project spaces respectful, inclusive, and focused on improving the software.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful.
- Discuss code and ideas without personal attacks.
- Explain disagreements clearly and consider feedback.
- Respect privacy. Do not post customer records, credentials, session data, or private communications.
- Keep project discussion constructive and accessible to contributors with different experience levels.
- Treat reports involving security, financial information, and personal data with care.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without permission are unacceptable. Repeatedly derailing discussion or using demeaning language is also unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces when someone is representing or participating in the SwiftRide project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer, Brajesh Kumar (@brajesh31), through the contact options on https://github.com/brajesh31. Do not post private details in a public issue.

The maintainer will review reports fairly and as promptly as practical, protect reporter privacy where possible, and may request further context. Depending on severity and circumstances, responses may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from project spaces. Outcomes will be communicated when appropriate.
