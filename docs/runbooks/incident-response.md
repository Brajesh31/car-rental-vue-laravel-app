# 🚨 Incident Response Runbook

1. Confirm impact and affected accounts, routes, bookings, uploaded files, and data.
2. Assign an incident lead and record UTC timestamps.
3. Contain by restricting affected routes, invalidating sessions, rotating credentials, or rolling back.
4. Preserve sanitized evidence in restricted storage.
5. Assess exposure of account details, sessions, rental history, private images, or secrets.
6. Restore database/files from approved recovery point when needed.
7. Verify role ownership, booking totals, overlapping reservations, mail, and scheduler behavior.
8. Coordinate notifications through responsible privacy/security owners.
9. Complete an incident report and remediation tasks.

Do not attach raw database dumps, browser cookies, APP_KEY, or personal records to public GitHub issues.
